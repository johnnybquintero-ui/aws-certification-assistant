# CLF-C02 Domain 1: Cloud Concepts

Exam weighting: 24% of scored content.

## Overview

Content Domain 1 covers Cloud Concepts and represents 24% of the scored content on the exam.

## Task 1.1: Benefits of the AWS Cloud

### Knowledge

- The value proposition of the AWS Cloud.

### Skills

- Understanding the benefits of global infrastructure, such as speed of deployment and global reach.
- Understanding the advantages of high availability, elasticity, and agility.

## Task 1.2: AWS Cloud Design Principles

### Knowledge

- The AWS Well-Architected Framework.

### Skills

- Understanding the pillars of the AWS Well-Architected Framework: operational excellence, security, reliability, performance efficiency, cost optimization, and sustainability.
- Identifying differences between the pillars of the AWS Well-Architected Framework.

## Task 1.3: Cloud Migration Benefits and Strategies

### Knowledge

- Cloud adoption strategies.
- Resources that support the cloud migration journey.

### Skills

- Understanding the components of the AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (AWS CAF), including reduced business risk; improved environmental, social, and governance (ESG) performance; increased revenue; and increased operational efficiency.
- Identifying appropriate migration strategies, such as database replication and the use of AWS Snowball.

## Task 1.4: Cloud Economics

### Knowledge

- Aspects of cloud economics.
- Cost savings from moving to the cloud.

### Skills

- Understanding the role of fixed costs compared with variable costs.
- Understanding costs associated with on-premises environments.
- Understanding the differences between licensing strategies, such as the Bring Your Own License (BYOL) model compared with included licenses.
- Understanding the concept of rightsizing.
- Identifying the benefits of automation.
- Understanding economies of scale, including cost savings.
